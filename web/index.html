ZK-Proofscan

Checks a zero-knowledge proof on this device and says what it does and does not establish. Nothing is uploaded.

It reads a Semaphore v4 proof, a RISC Zero Groth16 receipt or a Cairo proof from the Stone prover on its own. A Groth16, PLONK or FFLONK proof written by snarkjs, or a Noir proof written by Barretenberg, needs its public inputs and verification key as well. Noir proofs from Barretenberg 0.87.0, and looking a proof up by transaction, need the command-line tool.

Prototype. Unaudited, and the explanation wording has not been reviewed by a qualified person. Do not rely on a result from this page for a decision.

Report

The check runs in your browser, and the files you choose are not sent anywhere. Looking up a transaction sends its hash, and nothing else, to the public RPC named beside the button, which can see that someone asked about it; opening a shared link to a result does the same. Like any website, the company that hosts this page can see that you visited it and may set its own cookies. Public inputs in a proof can identify a person, so think before sharing a report or an export.
