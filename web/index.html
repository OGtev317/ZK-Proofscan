ZK-Proofscan

Checks a zero-knowledge proof on this device and says what it does and does not establish. Nothing is uploaded.

It reads a Semaphore v4 proof or a RISC Zero Groth16 receipt on its own, or a Groth16, PLONK or FFLONK proof written by snarkjs together with its public inputs and verification key. Noir and Cairo proofs are checked by the command-line tool, not by this page.

Prototype. Unaudited, and the explanation wording has not been reviewed by a qualified person. Do not rely on a result from this page for a decision.
